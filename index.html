Clinic Triage & Appointment Guide

Symptom / Reason for Visit Duration Triage Notes / Intake Questions
Abdominal Pain 60 Minutes Ask about duration, severity, fever, or nausea. Book with GP.
Sore Throat 30 Minutes Check for fever or difficulty swallowing. Standard slot.
Rash / Skin Condition 30 Minutes Ask if spreading rapidly or causing pain.
Prescription Renewal 15 Minutes Confirm medication name and pharmacy preference.
